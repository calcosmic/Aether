# Plan 148-04: Flagship Workflow Verification and Autopilot Pause Conditions

**Objective:** Verify all 9 flagship workflows produce correct state mutations and ceremony output. Verify autopilot respects all 10 Classic pause conditions.

## must_haves

- All 9 workflows (build, continue, plan, colonize, autopilot, seal, entomb, swarm, oracle) have at least one passing test proving state mutations
- Autopilot pause conditions are documented and tested
- Each workflow test verifies both state correctness and ceremony event emission
- Gaps from the research (missing entomb_test.go, missing autopilot_pause_test.go) are filled
