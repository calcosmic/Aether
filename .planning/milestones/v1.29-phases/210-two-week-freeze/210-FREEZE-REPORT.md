# Phase 210: the two-week freeze, report

Written 2026-10-05. Facts only. The verdict is the owner's and is recorded separately.

## What this was

The owner used Aether for real work in his own projects, and only things he actually hit were
fixed. The point was to find out whether the framework earns its place. The trial started on
Thursday 25 September 2026 and was due to end on Thursday 9 October 2026. **He chose to close it
early, on Monday 5 October**, so it covers eleven days, not fourteen. It ran on the Aether
program installed on his machine: version `1.0.88`, first built from commit `bcf60ed4`, and
replaced many times during the trial as each fix landed. No publish or release was involved.

The program measured did not stay the same. It changed by the owner's own choice on 26 September
(the clarifying interview), 27 September (the new screen layout), 3 October (Autopilot keeps
going and asks once; steps a locked-down helper cannot do go to the chat; the CI-file guard
applies only to Aether's helpers) and 4 October (the finishing step lists fixes instead of making
them; the program's own check failures never become rules). Details are in the start record.

## What this cannot tell you

One person, one machine, eleven days: a real result, not a statistic. The count holds only what
he brought to Aether's chat. Anything he dealt with inside a project's own chat without a row
being written is missing, and a missing row makes the verdict kinder than it should be. When asked
on 2026-09-28 whether the ledger was complete, he said "i dunno". Most of the stops came in two
bursts: French Fluency on 25-27 September, and the Finish the Track deck on 2-3 October. Several
rows are one long bad session rather than separate days. The trial was also closed four days early,
so the most recent fixes go into the verdict mostly untried in real work.
