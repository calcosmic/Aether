<objective>
Turn the stub that has allowed every spawn it has ever seen into the real decision, and call it
from the recorder so a refusal leaves no trace.

Purpose: `spawnCanSpawnDecision` at `cmd/spawn.go:176` was built for exactly this phase — a
package-level function variable, already swappable by tests, already wired to `--enforce`'s
non-zero-exit path. Only the decision itself is missing. Widening it in place, rather than adding
a second seam, is what keeps depth, budget and ancestor-cycle answering through one chokepoint.

Output: a depth cap of two levels of delegation (D-01/D-05), enforced at both the courtesy
pre-check and the authoritative recorder, plus the two check-function contracts plans 05 and 06
fill in.

Scope boundary: this plan writes `cmd/spawn_budget.go` and `cmd/spawn_ancestor.go` as *contracts
only* — correct signatures, wired call sites, bodies that return no reason and carry an explicit
marker comment. Plan 05 fills the budget body, plan 06 fills the ancestor body, and plan 10
asserts no marker survives. Defining the contracts here is what lets those two plans run in
parallel without touching `cmd/spawn.go`.
</objective>
