<objective>
Make the first seven lifecycle surfaces end with the shared card instead of
their own hand-written advice.

Purpose: criterion 2 asks that every command's closing message come from the one
shared logic. These seven are where the owner spends most of a project, and they
carry the largest hand-written blocks — the starting card alone hard-codes three
alternatives that are identical for every repository on earth. Replacing them is
what turns the resolver from a library into the thing the owner actually reads.
Output: seven migrated closings, seven envelopes carrying the same fields, and
the golden transcripts updated to match.
</objective>
