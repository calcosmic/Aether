<objective>
Plain English first: three things here. First, when you tell the system "I already did this by hand", that now counts as real proof on both ways of running the check step — today it counts on one and is ignored on the other, which leaves people stuck. Second, when a helper says "I ran these commands and changed these files", the program re-runs those commands and looks for those files itself instead of taking the helper's word. Third, when a requirement genuinely cannot be proved by any machine, it is marked as needing your say-so: the work still moves forward, but filing the project away waits until you have answered.

Technically: completes FLOOR-03. Closes the folded 2026-08-01 todo (`--reconcile-task` ignored by the finalize lane's implementation-evidence gate), implements D-04's builder-evidence re-run by reusing the `verify-out-of-band` re-verify core, and adds D-05's `needs_owner_confirmation` criterion state wired to the existing seal and decision-answer paths.

Purpose: remove the last places where a gate implicitly demands a reviewer worker.
Output: reconciliation as first-class evidence on both lanes, a program-run evidence check, an owner-confirmation state, and `TestGateAcceptsDeterministicEvidenceWithoutReviewer`.
</objective>
