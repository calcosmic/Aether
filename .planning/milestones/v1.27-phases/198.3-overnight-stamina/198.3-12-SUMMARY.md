## Decisions Made

- Treat only `errors.Is(err, os.ErrNotExist)` as an empty owner-work store. Any other read or decode error is evidence that seal cannot prove safety.
- Reserve `pending-decisions-storage-unavailable` for the synthesized hard blocker so an ordinary flag collision cannot hide a trust-boundary failure.
- Deduplicate compatibility projections by the checkpoint's stable identity rather than its rotating command/capability.
- Persist only capability hashes and stable identifiers; return raw capabilities only through immediate command output.
- Route durable status/report next actions back through `aether seal`, where a fresh capability can be issued safely.
