## Decisions Made

- The compatibility key remains stable across generations and is used only to match the computed legacy seal projection. The durable row ID and capability authorization derive from compatibility plus `WorkGeneration`.
- Runtime evidence hashes normalized criterion identity, policy/classification/result, summary, evidence, required checks, blocking issues, and artifact source paths. Criteria and set-like slices are sorted; timestamps, renderer text, and capabilities are excluded.
- Direct build final-status manifests may omit attempt fields only when they exactly match the latest journal-bound manifest's path, phase, root, timestamp, execution owner, and claims path. The journal's immutable manifest remains the authorization source.

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Accepted the legitimate direct-build final projection without weakening provenance validation**
