## Decisions Made

- A lesson is actionable only when its learning evidence is newer than the active plan revision, has valid phase/content/timestamps, comes from completed workers, and has no missing or failing quality gates.
- Replan cadence is necessary but not sufficient in unattended mode: at least one confirmed post-revision lesson is also required.
- Headless queue-and-continue paths first verify the pending decision is durable; terminal pause helpers are reserved for actual stops.
- Current typed evidence is authoritative. Historical text markers, stale gate scores, old escalation flags, and pre-existing ordinary blockers do not independently stop the run.
- The run loop invokes one whole build per phase. Provider readiness owns its own bounded timeout retry, preventing duplicated retry budgets.
