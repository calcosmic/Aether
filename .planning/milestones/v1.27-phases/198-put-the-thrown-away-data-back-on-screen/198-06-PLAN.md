<objective>
Put the checks, the gates and the evidence on screen. The closing summary currently says
"Verification: 3 passed, 1 skipped" and "Gates: 5/6 passed" and stops — the names, the
outcomes, the fix hints and the proof behind every claimed requirement are all computed and
all discarded, on the direct path as well as the chat path.

Purpose: this is the heart of SHOW-02 and of D-11. These items are not a chat-path parity
problem — the direct path does not show them either — so this is new rendering written once
in the shared renderer, which both paths then get by construction.

Output: three new render helpers in the shared renderer plus tests that fail on a bare tick,
on a silent truncation, and on a dropped finding block.
</objective>
