<objective>
Make the pre-build card tell the truth about who is being sent and why. Two
defects are fixed together because they live in the same render loop: the card
currently falls back to the roster's generic description of a caste when no
per-phase reason exists — which reads as a justification and is not one — and it
marks workers REQUIRED without saying what required them.

Ordering rationale: this plan owns `cmd/ceremony_team_checkin.go` and edits
`cmd/codex_build.go`, which plans 194-01 and 194-03 also edit; and it renders the
per-caste reasons that plan 194-03 produces and the forced-reviewer records that
plan 194-01 writes. It cannot run before either. Plan 194-05 is kept in a later
wave rather than beside this one even though their file sets are disjoint,
because this plan's REQUIRED fixtures encode the required-caste set that 194-05
changes — running them in parallel would pin a set that is about to move.

Purpose: D-09, D-10, the card half of TEAM-03 and the announcement half of TEAM-02.
Output: a card whose reason column is per-phase sentences and whose REQUIRED
marking is always explained.
</objective>
