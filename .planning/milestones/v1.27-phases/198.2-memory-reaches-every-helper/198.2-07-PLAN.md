<objective>
Tell the next phase's builder what happened in the phase before it — what failed, what was flagged, and the closing summary the owner read — without handing over the full reports.

The records exist: `build/phase-N/verification.json` and `build/phase-N/review.json` are written by both check lanes, and plan 05 adds `build/phase-N/outcome.md`. Today the only reader of those files is the owner-confirmation seal blocker (`criterion_owner_confirmation.go:121`) and the seal's final review (`seal_final_review.go:885`). No build brief has ever read any of them. The next builder starts each phase blind to why the last one struggled.

Purpose: the most useful thing a new builder can know is what went wrong last time.
Output: a bounded previous-phase carry-forward section in the build brief, plus the measurement that proves this whole phase did not make briefs sprawl.
</objective>
