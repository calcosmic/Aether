<objective>
Show the research helper the shared cross-project lessons it is currently asked to summarise from nothing.

`renderPhaseResearchBrief` (cmd/phase_research.go:123-165) reads no shared lessons at all, yet its output template instructs the helper to fill a shared-lessons section; when no research helper runs, the fallback template at cmd/codex_plan.go:~3386 prints a hardcoded stand-in sentence under that same heading. Both are asking for, or faking, content nobody was given.

Purpose: a helper told to summarise something it cannot see will invent it.
Output: the same top-5 selection every other helper receives, rendered into the research brief, with the invent-it instruction and the stand-in sentence both gone.

This plan was split out of plan 06 (see that plan's scope note). It runs in wave 3 because it edits `renderPhaseResearchBrief` and `cmd/codex_plan.go`, both of which plan 06 also edits — the two cannot run side by side. Plan 07, the other wave-3 plan, touches no file this plan touches.
</objective>
