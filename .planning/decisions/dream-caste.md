# COLONY-05 — Dream stays a command, not a 28th caste

**Decision date:** 2026-08-16
**Status:** settled.

Phase 166 required a written decision on whether Dream becomes a 28th caste or
remains a command. The decision: **Dream remains a command** (`/ant-dream`).

Reasoning, per the roadmap's own correction: a Dream *worker* cannot exist —
the earlier "Sage and Dream at milestone close" phrasing implied a spawnable
worker, but dreaming is a session-level reflective act the operator invokes,
not a task a worker completes with verifiable output. A caste needs a job whose
result can be checked (the Definition of Done); a dream deliberately has none.
The 27-caste roster stays as it is; `/ant-dream` and `/ant-interpret` stay as
commands. Nothing spawns a Dream worker, and the caste registry tests continue
to pin the roster.
