## Tests

### 1. Guided Help and Current Standing
expected: Opening the ordinary Aether help shows the current colony standing first, then three clearly separated groups: Normal journey, Steer and inspect, and Expert maintenance. With no colony it says "No colony is active" and points to `/ant-init "goal"`; with an active colony it shows that colony's real standing. The screen does not dump internal protocol commands or expose retired pause-colony, resume-colony, recover, or abandon commands.
result: pass

### 2. Guided Init and Automatic Setup
expected: In a new repository, `/ant-init "goal"` gives a Queen-led opening, performs safe setup automatically, repeats the accepted goal and material constraints, reports territory freshness, persists the accepted charter, and closes with exactly `/ant-plan`. Invoking init when a colony is already active refuses before changing state and names the safe existing-colony route.
result: pass

### 3. Automatic Territory and Planning Handoff
expected: Planning reuses a fresh territory survey, refreshes missing or stale evidence automatically, and stops with a specific recovery route when survey truth is unavailable. The accepted plan is tied to the verified territory snapshot. Once planning is accepted, guided build and Autopilot are presented as equal choices, with neither labelled recommended.
result: pass

### 4. One Authoritative Orientation Model
expected: Full status presents colony/goal, phase and tasks, real active ants and lineage, standing instructions, territory/research/Dreams, memory/findings/verification, elapsed time and reported cost, recent history, blockers or unknowns, and Next Up in a stable order. Compact status remains a truthful short subset. Phase and history agree with status on identity, standing, blockers, decisions, and Next Up, and merely viewing any of them does not change repository state.
result: pass

### 5. Honest Autopilot and Idle Watch
expected: Starting Autopilot without a colony or accepted plan refuses with the missing prerequisite, says state is unchanged, and points exactly to init or plan. A valid run shows its goal, phase range, active standing instructions, allowed revisions, and pause boundaries, then starts without a second confirmation. Repairs are bounded and receipted, debt stays visible, independent safe work can continue, completion never auto-seals, and an idle Watch says no ants are active while showing actual recent status rather than fake liveness.
result: pass

### 6. Evidence-Backed Steering and Scoped Swarm
expected: Focus, feedback, and redirect return a durable receipt that distinguishes delivery, acknowledgement, measured effect, and effect on active work. When live delivery is unavailable, the output honestly says next safe boundary or unsupported instead of claiming influence. Redirect affects only proven conflicting work, and Swarm localizes its intervention while plainly identifying any Phase 202 live-event limitation.
result: pass

### 7. Safe, Exactly-Once Pause
expected: `/ant-pause` waits for or identifies a safe boundary, records one validated handoff containing partial work, attempt, decisions, context, repository/worktree evidence, worker lineage, blockers, signals, and restart point, then shows one transaction receipt and `/ant-resume`. Replaying the same pause retains the existing handoff and does not duplicate decisions, workers, artifacts, cleanup, events, or receipts.
result: pass

### 8. Honest Resume and Recovery
expected: `/ant-resume` is the only public recovery door. It labels recoverable facts as Confirmed, Reconstructed, Conflicting, or Unknown; consistent evidence restores the safest runnable point with a receipt, while conflicting or insufficient evidence makes no runnable-state change and names what the owner must resolve. Old recovery names stay out of help and ordinary guidance, and expert recovery inspection is read-only and routes back to resume.
result: pass

### 9. Verified Versus Forced Seal
expected: Normal seal first shows evidence, gates, owner checkpoints, residual risk, rollback information, and what will be retained, then asks the owner to confirm. A forced incomplete seal is reachable only through a direct owner command with `--force --reason`, clearly says completion was not verified, records every unresolved item and the reason, and never uses normal success language or celebration. Both outcomes retain active sealed state for review, make status the primary next step, and keep entomb optional; the forced marker remains durable everywhere later shown.
result: pass

### 10. Archive Verification Before Entomb Clears State
expected: `/ant-entomb` refuses unsealed, inconsistent, or unverifiable closure state without clearing anything. For valid sealed state it previews the archive and active state to be cleared, asks the owner to confirm, then stages the archive, writes and verifies digests/cross-references, publishes the chamber and tombstone, and only then clears active state. Failure retains the active colony with a safe recovery route; success shows the chamber, manifest digest, receipt, retained memory/force truth, and `/ant-init "next goal"`.
result: pass

### 11. Expert Maintenance Is Safe and Separate
expected: Expert maintenance is visibly separate from the normal journey. Integrity, source-parity, archive, recovery, and live skill inspect/diff operations report current evidence without writing. Update, migration, cleanup, registry, binary, archive, or context repairs show exact scope and preview first, require confirmation where destructive, commit through one transaction, preserve unmanaged/custom files, and return either a verified receipt or an explicit no-change/rollback/recovery-required result.
result: pass
