<objective>
Let depth buy a heavier researcher, but only where it is earned: when a phase's
research loop stalls below target at deep or exhaustive depth, escalate that one
phase to an Oracle with a stated reason, and let Oracle's own RALF loop do the
digging.

Purpose: D-04 ("Scout by default, Oracle on escalation") is the last unbuilt
piece of the research iteration story. The escalation must not silently broaden
permissions and must not nest a second confidence loop around a loop Oracle
already has.
Output: a Go verb that builds the escalation dispatch, a scoped oracle
permission restriction, and the TS trigger.
</objective>
