<objective>
Give the run a ceiling that depth alone cannot supply, and tell the operator before they hit it.

Purpose: depth is not a budget. Eight workers wide and two levels deep is 73 workers while never
once breaking the depth rule. D-02 fixes the whole-run helper budget at 20, D-03 makes it count
every helper in the run including the coordinator's own workers ("a limit you have to do
arithmetic on to understand is a limit that gets misread"), and D-04 requires it to stay a
visibly different quantity from the Queen's per-wave cap of 4 to 8.

Output: the body of `spawnTreeBudgetReason`, whose contract plan 04 already wired into the one
decision function; a midden entry when the ceiling is actually reached; and a single plain-English
warning line at 75% of budget so the operator does not have to watch a second window.

Out of scope, explicitly: making the budget configurable. D-02 declined it — *a limit you can
raise under pressure is a limit that gets raised.* Do not add a config key, a flag, or an
environment override for the value 20.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `spawn-tree.txt` and the run-state file → budget count | Written concurrently by every worker in a run; a partial or corrupt read is normal operating condition |
