<objective>
Give the check step and the autopilot the same honest closeout the build now has: a real verdict, one recommended next action, and the cost and elapsed time for that exact attempt.

Purpose: the cost-and-time block is deliberately gated on a closeout carrying a work verdict, and the autopilot's own closeout has never carried one, so a full `aether run` ends with no report of what it cost or how long it took. The check step has the same hole for the verdict and the recommendation. Both lanes already reach a shared decision that knows the answer; nothing has ever asked it.
Output: one check-verdict derivation at the shared decision, one autopilot-verdict derivation from the recorded stop code, and both wired into the closeouts a person actually sees.
</objective>
