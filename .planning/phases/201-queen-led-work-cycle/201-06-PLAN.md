<objective>
Put elapsed time next to reported cost at the end of every closeout, and give the status dashboard the running colony total.

Purpose: The owner's standing complaint is that a one-plan job takes about thirty minutes, and until now nothing told him that at the end of a run. D-06 makes each attempt state what it cost and how long it took, and makes the total visible in one place, so the speed work later in this phase has a number to move rather than an impression.
Output: One cost-and-time block per closeout, an honest unmeasured sentinel, and a running total that provably equals its own rows.
</objective>
