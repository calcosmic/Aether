<objective>
Record what each build attempt actually produced: which files were credited, which were left orphaned, and what its workers decided and learned.

Purpose: the credited/uncredited split is attached only on the wrapper lane, and the decision/learning deltas are attached nowhere at all — the setter has never been called by anything but its own test. The autopilot lane and the direct build lane both seal attempts carrying neither. Without this evidence on the attempt, the cards the next plan renders would have nothing honest to show.
Output: both build lanes attach both pieces of evidence to the exact attempt they just sealed, derived from what really happened.
</objective>
