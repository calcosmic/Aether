<objective>
Make one job identity and one attempt identity carry all the way through the work cycle, and move claims and verification artifacts onto exact attempt-bound paths without weakening validation.

Purpose: The phase's third success criterion requires the Queen card, jobs, dependencies, waves, workspaces, receipts, findings and fan-in to refer to the same durable model. A second computation of which job owns a file, anywhere downstream, is a parity bug waiting to happen — and root-level artifact filenames that several attempts share are the same problem written to disk.
Output: One planning result read verbatim everywhere, an AST guard against a second derivation, and attempt-bound artifact paths with validating legacy reads.
</objective>
