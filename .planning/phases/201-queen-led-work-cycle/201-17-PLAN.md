<objective>
When the one automatic repair fails, tell the owner what broke, what was tried, where the project now stands, and the single thing to do next.

Purpose: the four-part handback is written and correct but nothing calls it. Today a failed automatic fix restores the checkpoint, prints one restoration line, and leaves the owner with a blocked screen that never explains what was attempted or why it did not take. The diagnosis, the attempt, the restored position and the one action all already exist as data — they just never reach a screen.
Output: the handback assembled from the real repair outcome inside the production check-repair path, carried on the check's own verification report, and rendered on the blocked check screen both continue lanes share.
</objective>
