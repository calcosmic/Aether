<objective>
Spend the measured time budget on the three levers the owner approved, and on nothing else.

Purpose: The largest term in a one-plan job is the test run, and the second largest is everything a worker has to read before it can start. D-15 approves exactly three ways to attack that: run the relevant tests during the loop, give the worker less to read, and put mechanical roles on faster models with the bill visible. It rules out the fourth and most tempting one — more workers at once — because contention is where this repository's timing flakes came from.
Output: Loop-scoped test lanes with a full-suite boundary gate, slimmer briefs on the existing handoff shape, and per-caste model routing with stated reasons and visible cost.
</objective>
