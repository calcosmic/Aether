<objective>
Make the Queen's per-phase choice of where reviewer judgment lands a single validated, recorded fact.

Purpose: Build-side and check-side reviewer castes are two independently computed sets today, which is precisely why the same phase gets reviewed twice. A boundary that is derived twice will drift the same way. Recording it once, with a reason, is what lets plan 201-05 delete one of the two review passes safely.
Output: A pure reconciliation function, a durable attempt-bound record, and one read path every consumer must use.
</objective>
