colony-prime template accessors (`writeSectionHeader`/`fmtOrFallback`/`sectionString`) shared with
loader 1's `cmd/colony_prime_context.go` — see 191-CONTEXT.md's "File-overlap note." Handling both here,
sequentially, avoids any risk of two parallel plans touching the same file.
