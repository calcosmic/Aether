<objective>
Put the two checks in front of the release gate under a name an operator can read, and prove
they bite by breaking something and watching it go red.

Success criterion 4 is deliberately worded to rule out the easy version: "verified by
deleting a caller and observing the gate go red, not by reading the workflow file." A step
that exists in YAML and a step that actually fails are different claims, and this repo's own
audits are full of the first being mistaken for the second.

Purpose: WIRE-01, WIRE-02, WIRE-03 (D-15). Closes the phase.
Output: A named CI step, a test that stops that step from falling behind the tests it is
meant to run, a recorded red-then-green transcript against the exact CI command, and the
phase's own records corrected to the real numbers.
</objective>
