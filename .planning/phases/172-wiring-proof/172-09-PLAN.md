<objective>
Close verification GAP B: success criterion 1 has live counterexamples. The orphan
ratchet records caller evidence under a bare leaf command name with no parent path, so
a documented call to `aether host colonize` clears the unrelated top-level `aether
colonize`, and `aether ceremony closeout` clears the unrelated top-level `aether
closeout`. Both top-level commands are registered, non-hidden, have zero direct callers
in any permitted corpus today, and are absent from the 278-entry live allowlist — the
ratchet believes they are wired. Twenty leaf names sit at more than one path, so the
same hole is latent across the whole tree.

Purpose: the criterion's central claim — "a subcommand with no caller makes the test
fail, naming the command" — is false today for two real commands. Keying evidence by
the resolved cobra command path (`aether host colonize` vs `aether colonize`) makes it
true, and a synthetic collision fixture keeps it true after those two are repaired.

Output: path-keyed evidence and orphan reporting; a hermetic collision test; a migrated
allowlist and baseline whose tolerance provably did not widen; a committed pre-migration
snapshot; corrected policy and roadmap records.

**Why three tasks in one plan.** The key-format change and the allowlist migration
cannot be separated across a plan boundary: between them the guard suite is red by
construction (every one of the 278 name-keyed entries stops matching). Task 3 is the
task that returns the tree to green; do not stop before it.
</objective>
