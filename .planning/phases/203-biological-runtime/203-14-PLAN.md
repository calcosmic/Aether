<objective>
Make everything the owner can look at cover the whole family, not just the first generation — and make the looking happen in the terminal they are already in.

Purpose: the owner's standing instruction from the previous phase's acceptance testing is unambiguous: they never use a second window, and liveness that requires one is liveness they will not see. Every surface this plan touches is therefore inline first. Underneath, the risk is the one this repository keeps hitting: building a second tree structure for recruits beside the spawn ledger that already records parent, depth and status for the whole run, and then having to keep the two in step forever.
Output: one subtree projection feeding status, the live view, recovery, cost and depth; one inline line per recruit and per refusal; one end-of-run family tree.

Declared dependency: Tasks 2 and 3 read the credit records and the `recruitmentCreditRecord` type that plan 203-12 creates, so this plan depends on 203-12 and runs in the wave after it. There is no stub or guard standing in for that type; the coupling is real and is declared rather than relied on by timing.

Flagged planner assumption (BIO-06's three classified edges are lifted into truths above; no unclassified BIO-06 row exists). The "follow-on edge" wording in BIO-06 is taken to mean the single named follow-on consumer a packet may address, which is the only follow-on relationship this phase creates.
</objective>
