<objective>
Make "this helped" a fact the runtime can prove: a recorded decision that changed, plus the verified outcome that followed — and make "this did nothing" and "this made it worse" equally recordable.

Purpose: the join this requirement needs is already written and already correct. `AgencyReceiptEvidence` carries a changed decision and effect evidence, enforces a both-or-neither invariant, and composes a measured-effect sentence. It has simply never been given real data, and beside it sits a constant still announcing that this machinery awaits Phase 202 — a sentence that became false the moment Phase 202 shipped and that nothing ratchets against. This plan is where that sentence stops being printed and starts being impossible.
Output: a credit record with an honest outcome vocabulary, populated from recorded evidence, with the stale limitation removed.

Flagged planner assumption (carried from the CEC-07 probe set; CEC-07's three classified edges are lifted into truths above): "verified outcome" is taken to mean an outcome the existing deterministic verification boundary recorded, not a worker's self-report. Surfaced here because a reviewer expecting a broader definition would widen what earns credit.
</objective>
