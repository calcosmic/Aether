<objective>
Give a child's useful findings a governed way home: a scoped packet, an explicit acknowledgement from whoever receives it, and a recorded note of what that receiver did differently because of it.

Purpose: the existing worker handoff relay already carries a summary from one worker to the next, which is most of the shape BIO-05 needs. What it lacks is the two things that make a handback *count*: someone confirming they got it, and a record of the decision it changed. Without those, plan 203-12's credit rule has nothing honest to read. Along the way, the word this requirement is named after has to be taken back from two commands that have been using it for error diagnosis and retry bookkeeping with no caller anywhere in the repository.
Output: a scoped acknowledged packet with a decision join, and two fewer known orphans.

Reversibility: **costly** — retiring `trophallaxis-diagnose` and `trophallaxis-retry` removes two published command names. What makes it undoable is that both are already recorded as known orphans with no caller, so the removal is provable rather than assumed, and the known-orphan list shrinking by exactly two is the evidence.

Flagged planner assumption (unresolved edge probe, BIO-05, category `unclassified`): BIO-05 says a packet reaches "an acknowledged parent continuation or one named follow-on consumer" without defining what makes a continuation acknowledged. This plan assumes acknowledgement is an explicit recorded act by the receiving worker or the runtime step that consumed the packet, carrying the packet identifier — not mere presence of the packet in a brief. Surfaced, not resolved.
</objective>
