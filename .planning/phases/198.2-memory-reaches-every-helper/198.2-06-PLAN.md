<objective>
Give the planner, the builder and the research helper the codebase map's actual content, and give the research helper the shared cross-project lessons it is asked to summarise.

Today the map reaches a brief as a list of filenames plus an age notice (`resolveSurveySection`, cmd/helpers.go:200-243). Seven survey reports — about 123 KB in this repository, dated 2026-05-19 — are never read into any prompt. And `renderPhaseResearchBrief` (cmd/phase_research.go:123-165) reads no shared lessons at all, yet its output template instructs the helper to fill a "Hive Wisdom" section from nothing; when no research helper runs, the fallback template at cmd/codex_plan.go:~3386 prints a hardcoded stand-in sentence under that same heading.

Purpose: a helper handed a filename has to go and read it, or guess. A helper handed the content does not.
Output: one deterministic map digest with its own budget and an age line, wired into all three briefs; and real shared lessons in the research brief, with the instruction to invent them removed.
</objective>
