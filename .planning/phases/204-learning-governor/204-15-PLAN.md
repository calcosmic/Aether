<objective>
Close verification gaps SC3a and SC5c.

Nine fields on the durable episode record — evidence identifiers, hard-gate results, changed decision identifiers, usage, reported cost, interventions, episode revision, acceptance digest and evaluator digest — have zero production writers. `emitColonyLiveOutcomeRecorded` and `emitColonyLiveInterventionRecorded` exist, are tested, and are called by nothing outside tests. Because of that, the two-figure improvement report classifies every real episode as unclassified: zero verified successes and zero preventable interventions on a live colony, by design rather than by bug, exactly as plan 204-10's own SUMMARY admitted.

Purpose: give each of the nine a real writer at the boundary that already knows the fact, register the ledger as the seventh store in the field-level census so the gap cannot silently reopen, and prove the report produces a real signal over episodes real lanes actually wrote.

Output: outcome-recording calls at the build, check and swarm closes; the ledger in `liveMemoryStoreCensusTypes`; a writer entry per field; and a live-data proof of the two figures.

**Depends on:** plan 204-13 (the closed intervention vocabulary and its three writers, and the swarm/recovery episode boundaries) — this plan writes the remaining eight fields and registers the census. Also on executed plans 204-03 (`cmd/memory_schema.go`) and 204-04 (`cmd/episode_ledger.go`).
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| worker-reported usage → durable cost figure | A worker's own reported tokens become a permanent cost record |
| lane gate outcome → verified-success figure | A gate result decides whether an episode counts as a verified success |
| census registration → suite verdict | The census is what stops a field going silently writerless again |
