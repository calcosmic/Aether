## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| lane entry point → durable ledger | Every lane writes an append-only record a later decision reads |
| owner action → intervention record | An owner's answer, waiver or release becomes a counted figure |
| worker/wrapper text → intervention kind | Free-form text must never become a category on its own word |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-204-13-01 | Spoofing | intervention kind | high | mitigate | The kind is a declared closed vocabulary validated at the single write point (`emitColonyLiveInterventionRecorded`); an undeclared value writes nothing (`TestUndeclaredInterventionKindIsRefused`) |
| T-204-13-02 | Repudiation | a lane that stops recording | high | mitigate | The skip branch becomes a named failure, so a dark lane fails the suite rather than passing quietly (Task 2) |
| T-204-13-03 | Tampering | duplicate intervention records inflating a figure | medium | mitigate | Intervention record identity includes its recorded instant; identical-instant duplicates collapse (`TestIdenticalInterventionsCollapseOnlyAtTheSameInstant`) |
| T-204-13-04 | Denial of Service | recording failure aborting a lane | medium | mitigate | Every new emission follows the existing warn-and-continue contract; the three owner paths keep their own tests green |
| T-204-13-05 | Information Disclosure | intervention text on a durable record | low | accept | Only a declared kind is stored, never the owner's free-form answer text — the vocabulary change removes this exposure by construction |

No package-manager installs occur in this plan; no npm/pip/cargo dependency is added, so no package-legitimacy checkpoint applies.
