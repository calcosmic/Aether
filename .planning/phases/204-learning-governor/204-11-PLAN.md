Write down what this phase actually changed, in words the owner can read, with a test beside every claim — and register the new behaviours as executable cases in the contract the next phase will check them against.

Purpose: this repository's own Definition of Done names the alternative by example. A documentation claim about runtime behaviour that is not testable has, three times in this project's history, described something that never ran. And the milestone this phase belongs to is closed by an acceptance phase that explicitly rejects label-only restoration — a phase whose behaviours are not registered as executable cases has nothing for that phase to check.

Output: one instruction-file section whose every claim names a live test, two byte-identical rule copies, an updated authority record, executable contract cases, and any remaining limit recorded rather than omitted.
</objective>
