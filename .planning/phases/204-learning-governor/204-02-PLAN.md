## Planner Assumptions (no CONTEXT.md — owner may veto)

**Assumption C — "did this help?" is answered by comparing the project's own checks before and after, not by asking the helper.**
When the system wants to know whether a piece of remembered guidance actually helped, it compares which of the project's own checks were failing before that guidance was handed over with which are failing after. A check that went from broken to working counts as helped. Nothing changing counts as no effect. Something that was working and then broke counts as harmed. The helper's own opinion of whether it helped is never the answer.
*If the owner disagrees:* the alternative is asking helpers to report it themselves, which the old system did and which this project's own rules already treat as untrustworthy input. Changing to that would mean removing the independent check.
*Technical:* outcome derives from `buildFreeCheckReport.Failed` on the phase's latest durable attempt compared with the same phase's previous attempt, both loaded through `listBuildAttemptsForPhase`. Absent a previous report, outcome is `pending` — never `helpful`.

**Assumption D — no decision recorded means no credit, not neutral credit.**
If nothing in the phase's own records shows the guidance changed a decision, nothing is written down at all. Silence is silence, not a score of zero.
