toolkit the four wrapper plans extend, and two permanent invariants that fail
loudly on regression — flat-mirror drift and retired depth vocabulary.

planner, in writing rather than by convention:

- **Open Question 1 (flat legacy mirrors):** resolved as Pitfall-1 option (a) —
  the flat mirrors `.claude/commands/ant-{init,plan,build,continue}.md` are
  git-tracked, two of four are already asserted by existing tests, and the two
  that no test covered (`ant-build.md`, `ant-init.md`) have already drifted.
  They are rewritten in lockstep with their canonical counterparts and pinned
  by a byte-equality test so the drift cannot recur.
- **Open Question 3 (does wrapper-host-contract.md need new content):** resolved
  as yes. A pointer to a document that does not contain the detail would be a
  paper claim, which the project's Definition of Done rejects. The manifest
  field shapes are authored there before any wrapper points at them.
- **Open Question 2 (terminal worker result: mechanics or method):** resolved as
  **method — stays inline in the wrappers**, and the ruling is written into the
  contract doc so a future reader sees the boundary, not a coin flip. Research's
  own recommendation agrees: the terminal-result list describes what a worker
  owes back, which is the opposite direction of data flow from manifest parsing.

Purpose: Wave 2's four wrapper rewrites can all point at a real contract section
and reuse one helper set, instead of each inventing its own.
Output: One extended doc, one new Go test file, two resynced mirror files.
