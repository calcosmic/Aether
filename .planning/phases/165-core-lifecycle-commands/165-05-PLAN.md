<objective>
Rewrite `init.md` (both platforms) onto the uniform D-05 stage skeleton, restore
the 👑 intention-setting beat the Classic charter ceremony had, and give init.md
its first dedicated ceremony contract test.

init.md is the one wrapper whose Classic ceremony genuinely lived in the wrapper
file itself (516 lines at v5.4.0, versus build.md's 65-line playbook stub), which
makes it the richest restoration target — and the most dangerous one. That same
v5.4.0 file is the origin of the CRITICAL D-2 regression: it reconstructed
COLONY_STATE.json with jq and wrote pheromones, constraints, midden, and
learning-observations by hand, producing the Frankenstein-state corruption that
forced all persistence behind `aether init --charter-json`. This plan restores
the file's banners, ordering, and approval copy while leaving every byte of
persistence in the runtime.

init.md is also the only lifecycle wrapper with no host-manifest step at all, so
its CMD-02 obligation is the strictest and simplest: zero envelope-parsing prose.

Purpose: colony creation feels like a ceremony again, and is explained as a
procedure.
Output: New init test file, rewritten init wrapper on both platforms, resynced
flat mirror, updated YAML guardrails.
</objective>
