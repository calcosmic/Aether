honest cost line in the shape the owner chose.

the two accounting paths were reconciled and after rows are actually being
written, because a cost line rendered over two disagreeing sources, or over an
empty ledger, is a confidently wrong number — and the owner's ruling throughout,
including the amendment to D-01, is that a number he cannot trust is worse than
no number.

The line's shape is the owner's own choice and is not open to redesign. Take it
from D-01 as amended in `196-CONTEXT.md`, which carries the exact worked example:
a total line naming the token count and the number of workers, then one indented
line per worker with its name and either its measured figure or a dash meaning
nothing was reported, then a footnote counting the workers whose tool reported
nothing. No figure is ever shown for an unreported worker, and the total counts
only the measured ones.

Render it from ONE function in `cmd/spend_cost_line.go`. Both workflows call the
same function; nothing else in the tree may render a second one. The line goes
last in the closeout, after the worker summary and before or after the next-step
block as the existing structure dictates — but exactly once per run.

Do not add a price table, a rate, or a currency figure anywhere. Plan 196-01
already removed the only currency relay from the ledger.
