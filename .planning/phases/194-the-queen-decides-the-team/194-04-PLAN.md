<objective>
Make the forced reviewer visible before anything spawns, and give the owner —
and only the owner — a way to say no to it, with a reason that is written down.
Today the card marks a worker REQUIRED without saying why it is required, and
there is no way to decline a reviewer the runtime forced short of editing code.

Purpose: D-03, D-10 and D-14, closing the owner-facing half of TEAM-02 and TEAM-05.
Output: a card that states the signal, a waiver recorded through the same path
the project already uses for owner answers, and a pause that still happens even
when the team is one worker.
</objective>
