<objective>
Retune the automatic path to the same floor the judgement path now sits on, and
make the owner's three dials mean exactly what the documentation will say they
mean. Today, with no proposal, the keyword engine picks the team by counting
word matches — that engine is what sent eight workers to a one-task bug fix.
After this plan the automatic team is the implementation worker plus any forced
reviewer, and the registry survives only to refuse proposals it can prove have
nothing to do.

Ordering rationale: this plan rewrites `cmd/caste_relevance.go`,
`cmd/queen_judgement.go`, `cmd/queen_spawn_budget.go` and
`cmd/codex_dispatch_contract.go` — every one of which plan 194-03 also edits —
and it removes the `t.Skip` markers plan 194-02 may have left in
`cmd/queen_probe_gating_test.go`. It cannot share a wave with either.

Purpose: TEAM-04 and TEAM-05.
Output: an automatic path that costs what the judgement path costs, and three
owner dials whose behaviour is pinned by tests rather than described in prose.
</objective>
