<objective>
Move the required-caste floor. Today an ordinary build is forced to carry a
verification caste, a coverage caste, a quality caste and often a security
caste, because the floor reads inferred phase mode, a blast-radius keyword
list, and the phase's position in the plan. This plan reduces the build floor
to the caste that writes the code, and replaces every implicit reviewer rule
with one explicit table of five named high-risk signals whose firing is always
visible and always explained.

Purpose: TEAM-01 and TEAM-02, and the first half of closing `.planning/WINDOWS.md` #1.
Output: a signal table, a shrunken floor, a forced-reviewer record that crosses
the build/continue boundary exactly once, and a retirement ledger for every
test that asserted the deleted rules.
</objective>

owner reads on the card.

(plan 194-02) asserts the generalised intent for every source, so a future
change that reintroduces a reason-less or arithmetic-reasoned worker on any
one path goes red.
