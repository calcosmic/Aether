**Signal:** `pluralization` on "fallback" — the phase introduces a second source
of a team where there was effectively one.

**Primary noun:** a *team decision* is now a list of `(worker, reason, source)`,
where `source` is one of `proposal`, `forced-by-signal`, or `fallback`.

**Decision: `promote`.** The keyword engine is demoted from "the thing that
picks the team" to one variant that must produce the same shape as every other
source — a caste plus a plain-English sentence. It does not get to keep its own
representation (a score-arithmetic string) alongside the new one. Rationale: an
add-alongside would let the fallback path keep shipping workers whose reason
slot holds score arithmetic, which is exactly what D-09 forbids and what the
owner reads on the card.

**Invariant test suggested and accepted:** `TestNoWorkerWithoutStatedReason`
(plan 194-03) asserts the generalised intent for every source, so a future
change that reintroduces a reason-less or arithmetic-reasoned worker on any
one path goes red.
</assumption_delta_decision>
