## Decisions Made

- Persistence durability is part of the command's success contract: retaining an in-memory outcome is diagnostic evidence, not a successful terminal result.
- The visual error headline must precede all outcome-like content; normal completion and terminal-decision renderers are suppressed after a failed final write.
- Dry-run may read the live typed lesson catalogue but must propagate catalogue corruption and must never create pending decisions or persist preview state.
- A pure evaluator combines lesson-aware eligibility, the narrow legacy interactive fallback, and canonical mode disposition for both live and dry-run callers.
