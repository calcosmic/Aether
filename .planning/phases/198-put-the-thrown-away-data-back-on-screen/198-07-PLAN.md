<objective>
Tell the owner, before a build starts, when something is genuinely stuck — and ask one
question rather than warning into the void or refusing.

Purpose: SHOW-02 lists a build-start blocker advisory among the data the runtime has and never
shows. Two of D-09's three signals are already computed by the check-in predicate; the third —
"the last check-and-advance on this phase ended blocked" — is recorded today only inside a
pipe-delimited event string, which is the wrong thing to read. The structured saved report
carries the same fact honestly.

Output: one pure policy, one predicate reading the structured report, the heads-up render, the
wrapper's one question in all three copies, and five lock tests.
</objective>
