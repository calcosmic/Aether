<objective>
Deal with whatever the day turned up: fix what can be fixed here, write down what cannot, and have the owner re-run only the pieces that failed.

Purpose: nothing is sealed with a hidden failure, and nothing forces a repeat of the whole day. A failure is either closed with a check or made explicit on the card the owner signs.

Output: a triage record routing every shortfall to a fix or to the card, any fixes made and released, and updated marks for the re-run pieces only.
</objective>

  <action>
Collect every shortfall from the day: each piece of work marked as failed; each marked worked-but-something-was-off, with the "but" extracted as its own item; each mark recorded as unsupported; each piece of work that left no durable record; and each timed piece that exceeded the limit even though the work was right.

For each, establish a cause by reading the program's own records — what was decided, where a person had to step in, how the run ended — rather than by reasoning from the owner's wording. He described a symptom; the cause is yours to find.

Then route each to exactly one of two outcomes, with the reason written down:
- **Fixed here** — the cause is understood, the fix is within this phase's scope, and a check can be written that fails without it.
- **On the limitations list** — the fix is out of scope for this phase, or the cause is an already-open entry in the defect register, or it needs owner input this phase is not asking for.

Nothing may sit between the two. An item that is "explained" but neither fixed nor listed does not exist as an outcome.

Where a shortfall matches an already-open entry in the defect register, name that entry. Where it is new, add it to the register with the same discipline every other entry carries, so the card can cite it.

Write `.planning/phases/205-owner-acceptance-and-restoration-seal/205-GAP-TRIAGE.md`: one section per shortfall with the piece of work it came from, the owner's own words, the cause you established and the record that showed it, the outcome, and the reason for that outcome. If all ten pieces of work passed with no reservations, write that plainly as the whole document and say that no re-run is needed.

## Flagged planner assumptions

- A re-run may itself fail. A second failure is triaged the same way, and if it still cannot be fixed inside this phase it goes onto the limitations list. There is no third round and no reopening of the whole day.
