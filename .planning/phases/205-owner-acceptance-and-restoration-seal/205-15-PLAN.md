Purpose: the acceptance bar is one mark per piece of work in his words plus an overall answer, and the times and costs behind those marks come from the program's own run record rather than anyone's memory. Three of the ten are also pass-or-fail on time.

Output: a read-back record of what the program logged, and the ten-mark verdict file the limitations card and the final verdict are built on.
</objective>
