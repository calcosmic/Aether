## Task 2 admission and disposition

No candidate supplies the required full settings/tools and complete observation-end source. A new full model/worker probe is therefore not admitted: it would repeat work without an established source for the blocking facts. The standalone prompt rendering and protocol generation above are discovery commands, not live qualification or a partial successful worker trial. Disk capacity is measured in the receipt but is not asserted sufficient for expensive work; no compile/resource preflight is claimed or bypassed.

Smallest next steps: (1) obtain a vendor-supported same-invocation export covering the missing full request and write-coverage boundary, then repeat this bounded acquisition; or (2) make an explicit, scoped architecture/proof-contract decision covering those missing facts and the retained native-launcher rule before planning implementation. Testing the optional app-server prompt/terminal or Claude forwarded-text fields can clarify partial evidence but cannot alone release Plans 25/26. No requirement reduction or control-plane implementation is authorized by this report.

Plan 24 remains incomplete; no SUMMARY or requirement completion is created. Plans 25, 26, 21, 22 and 23 remain dependent. Canonical verification remains `gaps_found`; the stable 1.0.81 hotfix and Phase 205 owner acceptance are separate and preserved.
