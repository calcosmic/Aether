Amended 2026-09-18 under [204.2-PROOF-AMENDMENT.md](204.2-PROOF-AMENDMENT.md), approved by the owner's explicit selection. Planning only; canonical verification remains gaps_found.

In plain English: prove the workers and recovery behavior Aether needs, record unavailable host internals honestly, and make Aether refuse operations it cannot safely guarantee. A refusal is evidence that a guard works; it is not evidence that cancellation succeeded.

## Why this amendment exists

The original acquisition pass required complete internal settings/tools and host-authored post-cancel write coverage on both hosts before any dependent work. Discovery and the completed offline collector did not establish those exports. Original ANT-04–06 still require actual governed workers, complete context/answers and durable recovery; ANT-07 permits unavailable capabilities to be refused. The approved amendment corrects the later evidence conjunction without waiving those mandatory behaviors.

Preserve all previous plans 01–20, summaries, review/verification history, original ACQUISITION and COLLECTOR-FEASIBILITY reports, the collector's completed 19-test result, and every historical raw/derived receipt. They retain their original meanings. The new contract never transforms a historical incomplete result into a success. Do not repeat the six-candidate discovery or unchanged expensive probes.

## Admission and acceptance contract

Plan 24 writes new `204.2-PROSPECTIVE-ACQUISITION.md` and `evidence/proof-amendment/admission.json`. It separates per-task `implementation_admitted` from `live_admitted`. Existing `ready_for_adapter=false` in historical receipts is unchanged; there is no replacement blanket requirement for twelve successful exports. Contract preparation has no live success or ANT credit.

- Mandatory worker, independent review, exact context/current-answer delivery and actual child ACK, core fresh-session recovery and exactly-once credit retain source-bound proof. Ciphertext, reconstructed expected bytes, parent work, invented ACKs or an unsupported label cannot satisfy them.
- Actual client executable/version/argv, reported model/effort, source/installed/corpus identity and immutable raw inventory remain required provenance. Complete internal settings/tool definitions may be explicitly unavailable. A positive export claim still requires its real supported source, semantic validation and same-invocation identity; a fabricated or unrelated artifact never passes.
- The runtime must expose a cancellation-guarantee request through its existing capability-admission path and refuse an unavailable guarantee before reservation/launch mutation. Unspecified requests preserve ordinary supported admission. This prelaunch refusal is separate from active-child safety.
- For a real cancel_requested/unknown child, cancellation acceptance requires actual supported evidence or actual public/runtime refusal of unsafe completion, recovery handoff and replacement launch. Capture original commands/results, exact child/attempt and before/after state/credit/spawn accounting. A test-authored refusal flag is insufficient. Unknown terminal state earns no cancellation credit and leaves `NoPostAckWrites=false`/unproved. Collector EOF, timer, quiet logs, cleanup and parent exit are not no-write or terminal proof.
- Optional capability support/refusal applies only to permissions/workspace/nesting/cancellation and similar host limits. Preserve real sentinel attempts where support is claimed. Missing ordinary/review/question/core-recovery behavior cannot be recast as unsupported.
- Claude remains one equivalent real installed `/ant-build` build/check journey with actual helper work, checks and durable credit from the same baseline. It needs genuine client/source provenance; it does not need every Codex context/cancellation acquisition row.

All eleven scenario names remain: ordinary, early-resume, review, partial-resume, question, cancellation, spawn-gap, controls, controls-read-only, missing-skill and claude. Existing passed versus observed outer outcomes remain honest; a nested capability disposition records supported versus actually refused. Mandatory passed cases cannot gain an unsupported shortcut.
