<objective>
Build the durable ledger itself: one record per phase, one row per worker dispatch, with the
measured/estimated split, the parent attribution, and the no-double-count invariant that make
every later number in this phase falsifiable.

Purpose: SPEND-01 says usage must outlive the process. SPEND-04 says an estimate must never be
readable as a measurement. SPEND-05 says the grand total must equal the sum of the rows. All
three are properties of this one file, and all three are asserted as properties rather than as
"the field exists".

Output: `cmd/spend_ledger.go` with the record types, save/load, totals, roll-up and the
`--include-estimates` gate; `cmd/spend_ledger_test.go` proving each.
</objective>
