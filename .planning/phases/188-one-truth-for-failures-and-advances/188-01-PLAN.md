canonical Go-internal helper pair (`loadMiddenFile`, `appendMiddenEntry`) that every consumer and
every future writer converges on.

consumers -- `checkAutopilotPauseConditions`, colony-prime's `buildContextCapsuleOutput`,

188-CONTEXT.md D-03).

`appendMiddenEntry` to record autopilot retry exhaustion -- that dependency is itself the clearest
possible proof this plan actually closed the loop.

Output: `cmd/midden_shared.go` (new canonical helpers), five consumer files repointed at it, two
existing test fixtures corrected (they were seeding the dead nested path), one new end-to-end
