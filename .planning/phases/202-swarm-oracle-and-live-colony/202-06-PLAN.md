<objective>
Build the live colony cockpit the owner actually asked for: one dashboard that updates in place, focused on the work happening right now, framed in the Classic colony's own voice, with every figure traceable to a typed event.

Purpose: D-01 and D-02 describe a screen, and D-04 makes its character a requirement rather than polish — the February-to-April colony felt alive, and the owner wants that back over truthful data. The risk here is the one the research names: building a beautiful renderer against an ad-hoc live-state struct, which demos perfectly and shows nothing when driven from the persisted file. Rendering only from the replayed snapshot is what prevents that, and it is also what makes the replay-backed idle view in plan 202-09 nearly free.
Output: `cmd/watch_dashboard.go` holding the dashboard and the ticker, wired into the live branch the tracer established.
</objective>
