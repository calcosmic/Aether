<objective>
Extend the one live-event model from the Swarm investigation wave to every remaining lifecycle lane — planning, building, checking, recovery and verification — so the cockpit shows the colony rather than one command.

Purpose: CEC-05 names six surfaces, not one. The tracer proved the model on Swarm; a cockpit that only lights up during Swarm would satisfy no owner running a build. Emitting at the real dispatch and transition sites, rather than re-deriving activity in the renderer, is also what keeps the "no invented activity" rule structural instead of a convention someone has to remember.
Output: Per-lane emission helpers over the existing boundary, call sites at the real transitions in build, continue, plan and recovery, and a coverage test that fails when a lane goes dark.
</objective>
