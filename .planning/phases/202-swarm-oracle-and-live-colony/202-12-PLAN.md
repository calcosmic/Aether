<objective>
Turn Oracle's output into an answer the owner can act on: the recommendation first in plain words, then how sure it is and what it could not settle, with the sources and the working underneath.

Purpose: A research run that ends with a wall of evidence and no stated recommendation has done the work and withheld the result. The owner reads top-down and stops when they have what they need, so the order of the document is the product. The counterpart risk is the opposite one: a run that stopped early producing a document that reads like a settled answer. Both are solved by the same discipline — lead with the conclusion, label its standing honestly, and keep every claim traceable to something the run actually found.
Output: `cmd/oracle_synthesis.go` holding the recommendation-first renderer and its provenance section, wired into the existing durable save path.
</objective>
