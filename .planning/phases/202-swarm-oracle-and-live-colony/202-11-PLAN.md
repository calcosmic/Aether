<objective>
Make Oracle's research watchable: emit what the loop already knows at the moments it already knows it, clarify the question once at the start, and then let the run finish on its own.

Purpose: Oracle is the most complete of this phase's three subsystems and the least visible. It already tracks the active question, the confidence, the open gaps, the contradictions and a consecutive-low-novelty counter that is diminishing-returns detection by another name — and none of it reaches anyone until they poll for status. The gap is visibility, not capability, so the correct move is emission at the existing mutation points rather than new state. The one behavioural change is the shape of the conversation: one clarification up front, then autonomy.
Output: `cmd/oracle_live.go` holding the emission, call sites at the existing mutation points, and a run that asks once and then works.
</objective>
