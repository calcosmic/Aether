<objective>
Build the one versioned typed live-event model this phase's every other outcome depends on, and prove it end to end on a single real path: a Swarm investigation wave emits it, a replay reducer projects it, and `aether watch` renders that wave live.

Purpose: Nothing in this phase can be "live" without this. The repository has already demonstrated the failure mode being avoided — `cmd/event_types.go` is a well-shaped event schema with zero production callers, dead since the day it was written. Building the emission first, at a real mutation site, and making the renderer a pure projection of replayed events is what prevents a dashboard that demos beautifully and shows nothing when driven from the persisted file. It is also what makes the replay-backed idle view of plan 202-09 free rather than a second implementation.
Output: `pkg/events/colony_live.go` holding the versioned vocabulary, `cmd/live_events.go` holding the one emission boundary, `cmd/live_projection.go` holding the reducer, and a `watch` command that resolves live, replay and idle in Go.
</objective>
