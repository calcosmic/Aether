<objective>
Make finished and unfinished work findable: the most recent episode on the status dashboard, every episode in the history listing, each with its outcome, its cost and a pointer to the full write-up.

Purpose: This phase produces real artifacts — Swarm episodes, research syntheses, partial notes — and the owner should never have to remember a filename to find them. The design risk is the one the research names by file: adding a third record type alongside the attempt record, the Swarm result and the research entry, which then has to be kept in step by hand. The correct move is one read-only index that reads the three durable records that already exist and presents them as one lineage.
Output: `cmd/episode_index.go` holding the shared lineage, with status and history both reading it.
</objective>
