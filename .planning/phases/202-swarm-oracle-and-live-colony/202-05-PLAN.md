<objective>
Give Swarm four genuinely distinct lenses and a first-class hypothesis comparison, so a stubborn defect produces a ranked repair with a stated reason rather than a concatenated wall of text.

Purpose: The current pipeline already dispatches several castes and already calls that an investigation. What it does not do is compare what they found. `renderSwarmFindingSummary` joins every worker's prose into one string and hands it to the fix wave — there is no step that weighs a claim against its evidence, notices two lenses contradicting each other, or says why one repair was chosen over another. That missing step is the whole of LIVE-03, and it is invisible until you look for it.
Output: `cmd/swarm_lens.go` holding a Swarm-owned hypothesis type, the comparison and ranking, and the single end-of-investigation card; and a Swarm pipeline whose fix wave is fed by that comparison.
</objective>
