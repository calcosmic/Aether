<objective>
Give Oracle the same four research-depth names planning already uses, each shown with its own confidence target and round cap, without touching the research economics underneath.

Purpose: The owner should meet one vocabulary across the product. Today planning offers Fast, Balanced, Deep and Exhaustive while Oracle offers a different cheapest-tier word and different targets, which makes two habits out of one idea. The trap here is treating the mismatched numbers as the bug: rewriting Oracle's 60, 85, 95 and 99 to match planning's 80, 90, 95 and 99 would change what Oracle actually does to fix what is only a display inconsistency. The decision explicitly permits the numbers to differ as long as each preset shows its own.
Output: `cmd/oracle_preset.go` holding the shared vocabulary over the existing depth map, and every owner-facing Oracle surface speaking it.
</objective>
